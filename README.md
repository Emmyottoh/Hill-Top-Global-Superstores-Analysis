# Hill-Top Global Superstores Executive Analytics Suite

## Project Overview
Retail executives require end-to-end commercial visibility to monitor multi-channel revenue, evaluate product category margins, and track regional sales performance across global territories[cite: 1]. This project delivers a multi-page interactive Power BI tracking suite engineered in two specialized design variants (Deep-Blue Executive & High-Contrast Dark Theme) to analyze global sales order workflows[cite: 1].

## Technical Stack & Tools
* **Business Intelligence & Visualization:** Power BI Desktop, Multi-Page Theme Design, Interactive Funnel Analytics[cite: 1]
* **Data Engineering & ETL:** SQL, Power Query, Data Cleaning, Normalization, Unpivot Transformations[cite: 1, 2]
* **Spreadsheets & Data Science:** Microsoft Excel, Python EDA Foundations[cite: 1, 2]
* **Core Competencies:** Analytical Skills, Statistics, Data Modeling, Retail Analytics, Stakeholder Communication[cite: 1, 2]

## Key Features & Business Impact
* **Executive Financial Architecture:** Constructed an enterprise retail BI dashboard tracking $437.87M in gross revenue, $27.39M total profit, and 6.98K orders across international sales channels[cite: 1].
* **Channel & Margin Optimization:** Applied **Analytical Skills** and descriptive **Statistics** to model payment method distributions, sales channel conversion rates, and product category profitability matrices[cite: 1].
* **Geographic Data Modeling & Funnels:** Engineered international market deep-dives using custom geographic visuals and tiered product line sales funnels to analyze territorial market penetration[cite: 1].
* **Dual UI/UX Design Standard:** Formatted high-contrast dark and executive deep-blue theme variants optimized for C-suite presentation decks and low-light monitoring setups[cite: 1].

## Data Pipeline & Methodology
1. **ETL & Data Cleaning:** Processed transactional order logs in **Power Query** and **SQL** to standardize currency fields, align country names, and eliminate order duplicates[cite: 1, 2].
2. **Data Modeling & DAX:** Applied star-schema **Data Modeling** and authored advanced DAX measures for profit margin percentages, rolling revenue, and YoY growth metrics[cite: 1].
3. **Stakeholder Communication:** Designed intuitive navigation pages to support executive **Communication** during commercial performance reviews[cite: 1, 2].
