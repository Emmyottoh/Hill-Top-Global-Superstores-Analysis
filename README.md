# Hill-Top Global Superstores Executive Analytics Suite — Power BI

## Project Overview

An interactive two-page Microsoft Power BI dashboard developed to analyze retail sales performance, product categories, payment methods, sales channels, customer segments, and historical sales trends across 2024–2025.

The project transforms retail transaction data into executive-level insights to support commercial performance monitoring and informed business decisions.

## Executive Dashboard

![Hill-Top Global Superstores Executive Dashboard](Hill-Top%20Retail%20Analytics%20Dashboard-BI.png)

## Key Performance Indicators

- **Total Sales:** $437.87M
- **Total Price:** $27.39M
- **Total Quantity:** 6.98K
- **Reporting Period:** 2024–2025

## Dashboard Structure

### Page 1 — Executive Financial Overview

- Total sales, total price, and total quantity.
- Sales performance by product category.
- Sales channel distribution.
- Interactive payment method filtering.

### Page 2 — Customer & Sales Trend Analysis

- Customer segment performance.
- Historical sales trends across 2024–2025.
- Comparative customer segment analysis.
- Sales performance monitoring over time.

## Technical Stack & Tools

- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop:** Interactive dashboard development and executive reporting.
- **Power Query:** Data preparation, cleaning, and transformation.
- **Data Modeling:** Organization of retail transaction data for analytical reporting.
- **Data Visualization:** KPI cards, column charts, donut charts, line charts, and slicers.
- **Business Intelligence:** Retail performance analysis and decision-support reporting.

## Analytical Objectives

- Evaluate overall retail sales performance.
- Identify leading product categories.
- Examine sales distribution across multiple channels.
- Analyze customer segment performance.
- Monitor historical sales trends.
- Support data-driven commercial planning.

## Business Value

The dashboard consolidates essential retail performance indicators into an accessible executive reporting environment, helping business decision-makers understand sales patterns, customer segments, product performance, and channel contributions.
